Taekwondo Classes

Uniform and belt are required for all Taekwondo classes. New students can train in athletic wear for the first session while their uniform is on order.

Kickboxing Classes

A Twin Tigers t-shirt is required for all Kickboxing classes. Bring your own gloves. Olympic Sparring requires full sparring equipment; Jiu-Jitsu Weapons requires your own weapons.

Holiday & Weather Closures

Schedules shift around major holidays and severe weather. We post every closure, make-up day, and schedule change to our socials first, so give us a follow to stay in the loop.
